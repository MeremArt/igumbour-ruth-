Honours Thesis Researcher · York University

Berberine, wall teichoic acid biology & β-lactam susceptibility in Staphylococcus aureus

Investigated how a plant-derived natural product influences cell-wall associated processes and antibiotic susceptibility in RN4220 wild type and ΔfmtA S. aureus.

  • Designed and executed microbiology experiments with bacterial cultures and controlled treatment conditions.
  • Performed antimicrobial susceptibility testing using broth microdilution / MIC workflows.
  • Prepared media and reagents, carried out serial dilutions, pipetting, aseptic handling, and experimental documentation.
  • Used WTA extraction, ¹H NMR-based analysis, D-alanine quantification, and phosphate normalization to interpret cell-wall changes.
  • Reviewed literature, analyzed unexpected trends, and translated findings into thesis, poster, and oral scientific communication.
